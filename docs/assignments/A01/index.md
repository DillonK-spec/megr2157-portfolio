### Task B: Product Analysis<br/>

Product: Nail Clippers<br/>
The primary purpose of nail clippers is to use a lever to concentrate the force applied an cut along its edge. The governing model essentially uses two moments, the force applied perpendicular to the lever multiplied by its distance to the fulcrum is equivalent to the force along the edge multiplied by its distance to the fulcrum. Assuming friction is negligible, the force at the edge will be greater since it's closer to the fulcrum.

<img width="4080" height="3060" alt="20260824_225351" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/34f0696e-16f1-43c1-b53f-bdad2ffbbc36" />
The left side of the assembly has the two cutting edges separated by a small gap with a pin behind it that acts as a fulcrum. The top of the pin is linked to the lever and as the lever is pushed downwards, the gab between the cutting edges decreases.

<img width="4080" height="3060" alt="20260824_225449" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/635b9f0d-7d03-4f2e-bcc9-3cd324308d06" />
The lever is a thin, wide piece of metal with a hole at the section that fits into the pin. The end with the hole is bent 45 degrees so it can act upon the cutting edge.

<img width="4080" height="3060" alt="20260824_225422" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/c062315d-d4fa-4903-91fc-04f34ad380ed" />
This piece acts as a jaw and has the cutting edge. It has holes straight through the body on both sides, the hole on the left holds the majority of the pin. There is a gap between the cutting edges that will close when acted upon. The other side of the jaw is closed and space between the top and bottom decreases linearly the farther away from the cutting edge.

<img width="4080" height="3060" alt="20260824_225430" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/ea755049-e9b6-4fc2-a8b6-1e46c6f163fc" />
The pin is designed to fit through the hole on the jaw and has a notch at the top that the lever can fit into. The other side of the pin is flat and wider than the rest, this prevents movement when the pin is in place. <br/>
<br/>

These nail clippers have a patent number US2664624A and were invented by William E Basset. This machine's purpose can also be achieved by by using two levers, similar to scissors, and adding a curved blade. Another option would be using something akin to a small sawblade to cut nails, the product could clamp to the nails then move against a surface. A deliberate choice with nail cutters is the curved cutting surface, it's designed to wrap around the edge of the finger or toe.
